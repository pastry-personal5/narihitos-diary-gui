## Final prompt

Create a charming hand-drawn editorial doodle infographic for a Mac diary app GitHub README. Wide landscape about 3:2, clean warm ivory paper background, dark charcoal wobbly ink, restrained sage green and apricot colored pencil washes. Lots of breathing room, large legible handwritten text. Three connected illustrated stations left to right with playful curved arrows: 1 a tiny voice waveform and scrap of handwritten notes with a little sun, caption exactly 'Bring a moment'; 2 an open rough draft page with pencil and two tiny magical sparkles to suggest AI assistance, caption exactly 'Find the words'; 3 a human hand holding a pencil adding a heart to a diary page, with a small growing stack of dated diary pages behind it, caption exactly 'Make it yours'. A whimsical looping arrow beneath the three stations returns toward the first, caption exactly 'A little today. More tomorrow.' The emotional idea: small everyday moments become a personal story over time, with human authorship central. Friendly imperfect notebook sketches, sophisticated minimal composition, no UI screenshots, no robot, no corporate clipart, no gradients, no photorealism, no other text. All captions must be accurate and readable at README width.
