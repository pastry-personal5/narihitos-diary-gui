## Scope

Included: Narihito mobile schema 1 ownership validation; bounded fresh preview; expiring one-use confirmation; Markdown-first native Trash; durable member receipts, step deduplication, reservations, restart reconciliation and renewed partial retry; fixed HTTP/Tauri operations; Explorer confirmation/results and resource-buffer guards; native synthetic and producer evidence; affected documentation.

Excluded: Ayumi deletion without its producer contract, bulk deletion, arbitrary attachment links, directory/catalog deletion, restoration, permanent deletion, metadata edits and automatic deletion. Existing source-save enablement does not enable Trash. The owner requires automated acceptance only: manual GUI, real-device and live-iCloud checks are not exit or enablement gates, including as deferred P1-M15 requirements at P1-M28. Their unverified behavior remains an evidence limit.
